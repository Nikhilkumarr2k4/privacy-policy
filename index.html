Privacy Policy

Comment AutoReply Bot · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Comment AutoReply Bot ("the app") is operated by Nikhil Kumar for the Instagram account @nikhilisobsessed. It automatically sends a direct message to people who comment on selected Instagram posts and reels. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

Information we process

  • Instagram comment data delivered by Meta's webhooks: the comment ID, comment text, the commenter's Instagram-scoped user ID and username, and the post or reel ID.
  • Message delivery status (sent, failed or skipped) and the time it was sent.

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details or any data from people who have not commented on our content.

How we use it

  • To send one automated reply message to a person who commented, containing the link or information promised in the post.
  • To avoid sending the same person more than one message per post and to respect rate limits.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private automation database (n8n) accessible only to the account owner. Logs are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

Your choices and data deletion

You can stop receiving messages by not commenting on our posts or by blocking the account. To request deletion of any data related to you, send a direct message to @nikhilisobsessed on Instagram with your Instagram username. We will delete it within 30 days.

Meta platform

The app uses the official Instagram API provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Your use of Instagram is also governed by Instagram's Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page.
